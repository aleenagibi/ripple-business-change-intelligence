CASCADE FREIGHT TECHNOLOGIES (CFT)
BUSINESS POLICY

Policy ID: RDP-002
Title: Rate Dispute Resolution Policy
Owner: Pricing & Rates Team (Marcus Webb, Director)
Approved by: Sarah Chen, VP Engineering; Diego Ramirez, Customer Success Lead
Effective Date: 2025-11-01
Last Reviewed: 2026-02-11 (revised alongside BRD-RE2-0091)
Related Systems: RateEngine, BillingCore
Related SOP: Rate Dispute Resolution SOP (SOP-RDR-02)
Related API: API-RE-007 (POST /v2/quotes/{quote_id}/dispute)

1. PURPOSE
Defines how CFT identifies, holds, and resolves shipper or carrier disputes over a
quoted or invoiced freight rate, and sets the freshness standard RateEngine must meet
before issuing a bindable quote.

2. RATE FRESHNESS STANDARD
Lane pricing data used to generate a quote must not be older than 15 minutes at the
time of issuance. RateEngine v2 enforces this automatically (BRD-RE2-0091 FR-5); any
quote built on data older than this threshold must be returned with status HELD and
error code RATE-409, never issued as BINDABLE.

3. DISPUTE INTAKE
Disputes may be raised by:
  a) A shipper, via CustomerPortal, which calls API-RE-007 POST
     /v2/quotes/{quote_id}/dispute.
  b) A carrier, via a Helix ticket routed to Carrier Relations, escalated to Pricing
     & Rates if the dispute concerns lane pricing rather than onboarding status.
Every dispute opens a case owned by Pricing & Rates and is worked per SOP-RDR-02.

4. HOLD CONDITIONS
A quote must be placed on hold (status HELD, RATE-409) rather than rejected outright
when:
  4.1 Lane pricing data exceeds the 15-minute freshness standard (Section 2).
  4.2 CarrierConnect API returns CARR-503 or CARR-401 during the required capacity
      check (see API-CC-003, BRD-RE2-0091 FR-2).
Held quotes must be resolved by Pricing & Rates within 4 business hours (Section 5.1).

5. RESOLUTION TIMELINES
5.1 Held quotes (RATE-409): 4 business hours to refresh pricing and re-issue or
    reject.
5.2 Formal disputes opened via Section 3: 2 business days for initial response, 5
    business days to final resolution.
5.3 Disputes resulting in a carrier-caused finding may trigger carrier suspension
    under Carrier Onboarding Policy (COB-001) Section 5.

6. FINANCIAL ADJUSTMENTS
Any rate correction resulting from a resolved dispute is written back to the
BillingCore rate ledger and, if an invoice has already been issued, triggers a credit
memo per Billing Operations' internal procedures (outside the scope of this policy).

7. REPORTING
Pricing & Rates reports monthly dispute volume and RATE-409 hold rates to Sarah Chen
(VP Engineering) and Marcus Webb (Director of Pricing & Rates) as part of the
RateEngine v2 success metrics defined in BRD-RE2-0091 Section 2.
