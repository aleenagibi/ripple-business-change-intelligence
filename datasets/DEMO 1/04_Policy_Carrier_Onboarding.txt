1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the requirements a freight carrier must satisfy before being
granted ACTIVE status in CarrierConnect API (see API-CC-003 section 4.2) and becoming
eligible for load assignment through Meridian TMS.

2. POLICY STATEMENT
No carrier may be assigned a shipment or appear in RateEngine's capacity checks
(API-CC-003 endpoint /v3/carriers/{carrier_id}/capacity) unless their CarrierConnect
status is ACTIVE. Carriers in PENDING, DOCS_REVIEW, or INSURANCE_REVIEW status will
return CARR-404 when queried by RateEngine, which causes rate quotes for that carrier
to fall back to RATE-409 handling per BRD-RE2-0091.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
Before advancing past DOCS_REVIEW, a carrier must submit, via CarrierConnect API
endpoint GET /v3/carriers/{carrier_id}/documents:
  a) Valid MC (Motor Carrier) Authority number
  b) Certificate of Insurance with minimum $1,000,000 auto liability coverage
  c) Completed W-9
  d) Signed Carrier Services Agreement

4. INSURANCE REVIEW REQUIREMENTS
Carrier Relations must verify insurance coverage meets or exceeds policy minimums
before advancing a carrier from INSURANCE_REVIEW to ACTIVE. Insurance must be
re-verified every 12 months; failure to re-verify automatically reverts a carrier's
status to SUSPENDED in CarrierConnect API.

5. SUSPENSION AND REACTIVATION
A carrier is moved to SUSPENDED status if:
  - Insurance lapses without re-verification (Section 4), or
  - The carrier receives 3 or more RATE-409 capacity-check failures in a rolling
    30-day window due to unresponsive systems (see API-CC-003 CARR-503), or
  - A rate dispute under RDP-002 is resolved against the carrier for cause.
Reactivation requires re-submission of documentation per Section 3 and sign-off from
the Head of Carrier Relations.

6. SERVICE LEVELS
Carrier Relations commits to processing a complete DOCS_REVIEW submission within 5
business days, and INSURANCE_REVIEW within 3 business days, per Carrier Onboarding
SOP (SOP-COB-01).

7. GOVERNANCE
This policy is reviewed quarterly by Carrier Relations and Platform Engineering to
ensure alignment with CarrierConnect API's capacity-check requirements introduced in
RateEngine v2 (BRD-RE2-0091). Any change to onboarding stages must be reflected in
API-CC-003 section 4.2 status values and in Team Responsibilities Matrix (TRM-01).
