CASCADE FREIGHT TECHNOLOGIES (CFT)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE / WORKFLOW

Workflow ID: WF-IR-01
Title: Incident Response Workflow
Owner: Platform Engineering (Sarah Chen, VP Engineering)
Systems Involved: Meridian TMS, RateEngine, CarrierConnect API, BillingCore,
CustomerPortal
Related Documents: SD-TMS-014, API-RE-007, API-CC-003, WF-SB-01

1. PURPOSE
Defines how CFT detects, triages, and resolves production incidents affecting any
system in the Meridian TMS ecosystem, and how those incidents are communicated to
affected teams.

2. SEVERITY LEVELS
SEV-1: Full outage of Meridian TMS, RateEngine, or CarrierConnect API; shipments
       cannot be booked. Target time to mitigation: 30 minutes.
SEV-2: Degraded performance (e.g., RateEngine p95 latency exceeds 4 seconds per
       API-RE-007 SLA, or CarrierConnect API error rate exceeds 5%). Target time to
       mitigation: 2 hours.
SEV-3: Isolated errors (e.g., elevated RATE-422 or CARR-404 rates for a single lane
       or carrier) not affecting overall availability. Target time to mitigation: 1
       business day.

3. DETECTION
Incidents are detected via:
  - Automated alerting on the SLA thresholds defined in System Documentation
    SD-TMS-014 (Section 4, Monitoring).
  - Error-rate spikes on RATE-500, CARR-503, or CARR-401 codes.
  - Helix ticket volume spikes flagged by Customer Success (3x baseline in 1 hour
    triggers automatic escalation to Platform Engineering).

4. RESPONSE PROCEDURE
Step 1: On-call Platform Engineer acknowledges the alert within 5 minutes (SEV-1) or
        15 minutes (SEV-2).
Step 2: Incident channel opened; Sarah Chen is paged for SEV-1 incidents affecting
        Meridian TMS or RateEngine.
Step 3: If root cause is traced to CarrierConnect API (API-CC-003), Platform
        Engineering coordinates with Carrier Relations to determine whether affected
        carriers should be flagged per Carrier Onboarding Policy (COB-001) Section 5.
Step 4: If root cause is traced to stale or incorrect lane pricing, Pricing & Rates is
        looped in per Rate Dispute Resolution Policy (RDP-002) Section 4.
Step 5: Customer Success is notified for any SEV-1/SEV-2 incident to prepare shipper
        communications and pause new Helix ticket auto-creation for the affected
        error codes to avoid duplicate tickets.

5. POST-INCIDENT
A post-incident review is required within 3 business days for any SEV-1 or SEV-2
incident, owned by the team identified as root-cause owner in Step 3 or 4. Findings
that require a policy or SOP change (e.g., adjusting the RATE-409 hold window in
RDP-002) are routed to the relevant policy owner for revision.
