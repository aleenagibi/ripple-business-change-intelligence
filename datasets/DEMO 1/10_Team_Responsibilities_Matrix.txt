1. PLATFORM ENGINEERING
Lead: Sarah Chen, VP Engineering
Owns systems: Meridian TMS, CarrierConnect API (API-CC-003)
Builds (not business-owns): RateEngine (API-RE-007), on behalf of Pricing & Rates
Responsibilities:
  - Production uptime and on-call for Meridian TMS, RateEngine, CarrierConnect API
    (see SD-TMS-014 Section 6, WF-IR-01 Section 6).
  - Implements FR/NFR requirements from approved BRDs (e.g., BRD-RE2-0091).
  - Owns Incident Response Workflow (WF-IR-01) execution.

2. PRICING & RATES
Lead: Marcus Webb, Director of Pricing & Rates
Owns systems: RateEngine business logic (rate rules, lane table)
Owns policy: Rate Dispute Resolution Policy (RDP-002)
Owns SOP: Rate Dispute Resolution SOP (SOP-RDR-02)
Responsibilities:
  - Defines and maintains lane pricing in the BillingCore rate ledger.
  - Resolves held quotes (RATE-409) within 4 business hours (RDP-002 Section 5.1).
  - Resolves rejected quotes (RATE-422) and lane requests.
  - Sponsors BRDs affecting RateEngine, e.g., BRD-RE2-0091.

3. CARRIER RELATIONS
Lead: Priya Nair, Head of Carrier Relations
Owns policy: Carrier Onboarding Policy (COB-001)
Owns SOP: Carrier Onboarding SOP (SOP-COB-01)
Responsibilities:
  - Reviews and approves carrier documentation (COB-001 Section 3).
  - Advances carriers through PENDING -> DOCS_REVIEW -> INSURANCE_REVIEW -> ACTIVE
    status in CarrierConnect API (API-CC-003 Section 4.2).
  - Manages carrier suspension and reactivation (COB-001 Section 5).
  - Provides carrier-side context during Incident Response (WF-IR-01 Step 3).

4. BILLING OPERATIONS
Lead: Aisha Thompson, Billing Operations Manager
Owns systems: BillingCore
Responsibilities:
  - Maintains the rate ledger consumed by RateEngine (API-RE-007).
  - Generates invoices at Shipment Booking Workflow Step 6 (WF-SB-01).
  - Issues credit memos following resolved rate disputes (RDP-002 Section 6).
  - Maintains 7-year financial record retention (SD-TMS-014 Section 5).

5. CUSTOMER SUCCESS
Lead: Diego Ramirez, Customer Success Lead
Owns systems: CustomerPortal (business ownership; Platform Engineering builds it)
Owns support content: Knowledge Base (e.g., KB-0142)
Responsibilities:
  - Tier 1/Tier 2 support via Helix ticketing system.
  - Shipper communication during held/rejected quotes (KB-0142) and incidents
    (WF-IR-01 Step 5).
  - Flags ticket-volume anomalies that can trigger SEV-2/SEV-3 escalation.

6. CROSS-TEAM DEPENDENCY SUMMARY
- Pricing & Rates depends on Platform Engineering to implement RateEngine changes and
  on Billing Operations for rate ledger accuracy.
- Platform Engineering depends on Carrier Relations for accurate carrier status data
  feeding CarrierConnect API.
- Customer Success depends on Pricing & Rates for timely resolution of held/rejected
  quotes and on Platform Engineering for system uptime and incident communication.
- All teams contribute BRD/policy review input; final sign-off on cross-system BRDs
  (e.g., BRD-RE2-0091) requires both the business sponsor and Sarah Chen as technical
  sponsor.
